## 🎯 About Me

I am a **Data Engineer** building **scalable data pipelines, analytics platforms, and AI solutions** that drive decision-making across **renewable energy, SMB, and public health** domains.

My work bridges complex data with real-world impact:
- Developing **production-ready platforms** using **Spark, dbt, and Dagster**
- Building **analytics pipelines** for EV products and battery energy storage systems
- Modeling **infectious disease transmission** to inform public health policy

I specialize in transforming raw data into actionable insights through robust engineering practices and modern data stack technologies.
